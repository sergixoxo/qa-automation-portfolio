
# US-001 — Shopping Cart Management

## User Story

As an online store customer,
I want to add products to my shopping cart and review them,
so that I can complete my purchase.

## Module

Shopping Cart and Checkout.

## Acceptance Criteria

| ID | Description |
|---|---|
| CA-01 | The customer can add a product to the cart from the inventory page. |
| CA-02 | The cart counter increases by one when a product is added. |
| CA-03 | The product button changes from "Add to cart" to "Remove". |
| CA-04 | The added product is displayed on the shopping cart page. |
| CA-05 | The product retains its correct name, price, and quantity. |
| CA-06 | The customer cannot complete a purchase with an empty shopping cart. |

## Business Value

Allows customers to review their selected products before completing a purchase and prevents invalid orders.

## Scope and Assumptions

This user story and its acceptance criteria are proposed for QA portfolio purposes and are not official SauceDemo requirements.
